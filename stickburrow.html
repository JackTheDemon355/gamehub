DIG DEEP · STAY HIDDEN · SURVIVE

HOW TO PLAY

🕳️ WASD / Arrows — Move your stick figure
⛏️ SPACE / Z — Dig a tunnel in the direction you're facing
💨 SHIFT — Sprint (uses stamina)
💎 Collect glowing gems to restore hunger & earn score
🟡 Yellow gems = shallow · Cyan = mid · Orange = deep (more points!)
🦊 Foxes chase on the surface — hide underground!
🦅 Hawks patrol the sky — run fast or dig immediately
🐍 Snakes follow your tunnels — they can dig too!
📈 The deeper you dig, the more score per second you earn
💡 Snakes can only enter tunnels you dug — walls block them.
💡 Hawks can't go underground at all.
💡 Dig charge refills when you stand still.
💡 Sprint only works above ground (stamina drains fast underground).
💡 Gems respawn over time — there's always something to find.
